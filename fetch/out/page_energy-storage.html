    Energy Storage

    Energy storage systems

    EEMENTOR ENERGY supplies LiFePO4 energy storage systems for homes, businesses and commercial sites. Choose from wall-mounted Powerwall units, rack-mounted modules, cabinet systems up to 230 kWh and all-in-one units with a built-in 5 kW inverter. Every model uses LiFePO4 cells for long cycle life and thermal stability, and communicates with your inverter over CAN or RS485. Pick a model, tell us the quantity and delivery address, and our engineers confirm compatibility, lead time and price.
