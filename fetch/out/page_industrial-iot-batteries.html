    Engineering focus

    What matters in an IoT battery

    Connected devices run for a long time on little power, often outdoors and in small housings. These are the points we go through with you before proposing a pack.

    • Runtime from your power budget

      Share your average and peak current and how long the device must run. Capacity and cell are chosen from that power budget.

    • Small and shaped to fit

      Cylindrical or LiPo pouch cells are selected to fit the space in your housing, with your connector and wire length.

    • Operating temperature

      Tell us where the device will be installed. Cell and chemistry are chosen for your operating and charging temperature range.

    • Charging source

      Solar panel, mains adapter or vehicle supply: the pack and BMS are matched to how your device is recharged.

    • Protection and monitoring

      The BMS provides protection and balancing, and can report battery status over UART, SMBus or Bluetooth where your device needs it.

    • From prototype to batches

      We can build a single prototype for your validation. Production scales from pilot runs to volume orders.

    What we can customise

    Every parameter, specified by you

    Share what you know and leave the rest to us. Our engineers will propose the cell, configuration and BMS that meet your requirements.

    • Voltage

      From single cells to high-voltage series strings.

    • Capacity

      Parallel groups sized for your runtime.

    • Chemistry

      Li-ion (NMC/NCA) or LiFePO4.

    • Cell model

      Named cells, energy or high-drain.

    • Current

      Continuous and peak discharge.

    • BMS

      Protection, balancing, smart features.

    • Communication

      CAN, RS485, UART, SMBus, Bluetooth.

    • Enclosure

      PVC, ABS hard plastic or steel.

    • Dimensions

      Fitted to your maximum envelope.

    • Connectors

      Your preferred connector and wiring.

    How we work

    From battery requirements to production

    Every pack starts with your application, not a catalogue. Your requirements go through a structured engineering process, with a validated prototype before any production run.

    1. 01

      Requirements

      Voltage, capacity, current, size, environment and interfaces, captured in one technical brief.

    2. 02

      Cell selection

      Named cells chosen for your current, energy, lifetime and cost targets.

    3. 03

      Electrical & mechanical design

      Configuration, interconnects, fusing, thermal path, enclosure and connectors.

    4. 04

      BMS integration

      Protection, balancing and CAN, RS485, UART or SMBus communication matched to your system.

    5. 05

      Prototype

      A working prototype built to the agreed specification, with no minimum order.

    6. 06

      Testing & validation

      Capacity, protection and temperature checks, then validation in your own application.

    7. 07

      Documentation & certification

      A complete battery datasheet once the specification is locked. Certification is arranged where the design, application and destination market require it.

    8. 08

      Production

      Batch production with quality checks and a test report for every shipment.

    Manufacturing & quality

    Manufacturing, documentation and quality

    We engineer your pack and manage its production, documentation and quality, so you deal with one technical partner from first spec to repeat orders.

    • Engineering-led, partner-built

      EEMENTOR ENERGY™ handles the engineering and project management. Production runs on the automated lines of our ISO 9001-certified contract manufacturing partner in China, with capacity for up to 400,000 batteries a month and volume lead times of around 30 days.

      For qualifying programmes with specific procurement or country-of-origin requirements, US manufacturing options are available through manufacturing partners.

    • NDAA

      NDAA-compliant configurations

      NDAA-compliant battery configurations can be developed for projects with applicable procurement requirements. Component sourcing, country of origin and manufacturing requirements are reviewed for each configuration during the engineering stage.

      Tell us your procurement requirements in your request.

    • Documentation & certification

      Our standard models are tested to UN 38.3 and IEC 62133 and CE marked, and our manufacturing partner is ISO 9001 certified.

      Certification requirements depend on the battery design, the application and the destination market. Typical examples are UN 38.3, IEC 62133, IEC 62619, applicable UL requirements, SDS/MSDS and battery datasheets.

      • Available now: datasheets and SDS/MSDS for the cells we use.
      • For a specific product: a complete battery datasheet once the specification is locked. Test and certification results apply only to the product configuration that was tested.
      • Arranged for your project: certification of a custom battery, at additional cost, which may be reimbursed once your total ordered quantity reaches an agreed level.

    • Traceability & quality control

      Every shipment goes through complete quality checks, and the quality test report is provided to you. Batches and cell date codes are traceable.

    • Custom packs for businesses

      Custom battery packs and engineering projects are for registered companies, from start-ups to established OEMs. Our battery cells are available to everyone, including hobbyists and DIY builders.

    • Direct engineering contact

      You work directly with the engineers designing your pack. Led by engineers with 10+ years of experience across battery systems, electrical engineering and industrial projects.

    How do you size a battery for an IoT device?

    From your power budget. Send us the average current, peak current, operating voltage and the runtime you need between charges, and we will propose the capacity and cell.

    Can the pack be charged from a solar panel?

    Tell us the panel and charge controller you plan to use. The pack and BMS are specified to match your charging source and charge current.

    We only need a small quantity. Is that possible?

    Yes for prototypes: we can build a single prototype. For production we scale from pilot runs to volume orders of up to 400,000 batteries a month, and we still support start-ups and innovative companies with small batches.

    Is there a minimum order quantity?

    Not for prototypes: we can build a single prototype. For production we scale from pilot runs to volume orders of up to 400,000 batteries a month, and we still support start-ups and innovative companies with small batches.

    How long does a prototype take?

    A prototype typically takes 25–30 days once the specification is agreed. Volume production typically ships around 30 days after order confirmation; we confirm the exact date in your quote.

    Do you sell to individuals and hobbyists?

    Yes, for battery cells. Anyone, including hobbyists and DIY builders, can order our battery cells online (minimum order 50 cells). Custom battery packs, energy storage systems and engineering projects are for business customers: registered companies, including start-ups and small companies.
